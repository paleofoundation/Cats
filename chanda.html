REAL PERSON · FULL-TIME CARETAKER · CYPRUS

Meet Chanda.

Chanda is the full-time caretaker of the Cat Gardens cats. The game introduces her later because the cats come first—but the real garden depends on a person who fills bowls, notices changes, gives comfort, and returns every day.

THE DAILY WORK

Care is repetition.

Food, water, observation, cleanup, companionship, and noticing when one cat’s behavior changes are not background scenery. They are the sanctuary’s daily operating system.

HER CAT

Datsun is pair-bonded with Chanda.

Their real relationship belongs in the Cat Gardens record. Future photographs and updates can be added here after sanctuary review without turning their bond into fictional game lore.

WHY HELP MATTERS

One person cannot multiply.

Volunteers and responsibly funded caretaker hours create more time for feeding, brushing, observation, petting, cleaning, and individual attention across a sanctuary of more than 90 cats.
