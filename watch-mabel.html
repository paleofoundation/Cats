Who Mabel is

Mabel—also called Mabel Fish—is shy with other cats but deeply sweet with people. She came to the sanctuary scratched and struggling after what appeared to be abandonment.

Her real care

Mabel’s higher-support routine includes special food, close daily observation, sanctuary-reported supportive care, and veterinary oversight. Dates, doses, provider notes, and receipts will appear only after review.

Why it is here

This film is part of Mabel’s Reality Portal: real media stays separate from game state, and care claims remain tied to sanctuary-reviewed information.
